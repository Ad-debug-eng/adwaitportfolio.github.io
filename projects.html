PROJECTS

IRC 2025 Rover – Team Automatons

Participated in International Rover Challenge 2025 at BITS Pilani Goa, achieving a 19th global rank. Contributed to the mechanical design and development of a 6-wheel rocker-bogie rover with a turret-mounted robotic arm for sample collection and manipulation tasks. Focus areas included chassis optimization, wheel geometry for improved traction, and structural integrity. Validated performance through CAD modelling, stress analysis, and iterative field testing.

Robocon 2025 – IIT Delhi

Participated in DD Robocon 2025 hosted by IIT Delhi, securing All India Rank 9. Designed and built a dual-robot system featuring multiple mechanisms for coordinated basketball gameplay — including dribbling, shooting, and passing automation.
